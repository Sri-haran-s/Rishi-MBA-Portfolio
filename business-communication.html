My key learnings

Module 1 — Communication as a Leadership Competency

  • Clear communication helps an audience understand the main point.
  • Preparation helps a speaker explain ideas in a structured way.
  • Examples and demonstrations can make an explanation easier to follow.

Add specific class or life examples in your own words.

Module 2 — Audience-Centred Communication and Stakeholder Mapping

  • Consider what the audience already knows and needs to understand.
  • Adapt the message to suit the people receiving it.
  • Use clear language so different audiences can follow the message.

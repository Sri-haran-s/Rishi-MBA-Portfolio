Why I Chose an MBA

Choosing an MBA after completing my degree in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science was an important decision for me. I wanted to learn more about business, management and how organisations make decisions. My technical background has taught me to approach problems analytically, while my MBA is helping me understand the people and business side of those problems.

I also want to improve my communication. Having good ideas is important, but being able to explain them clearly is equally important. Through my MBA journey, I hope to become more confident in presentations, teamwork and professional conversations.

I see this journey as an opportunity to learn, improve and prepare for my future career.

These talks offer useful ideas on marketing, entrepreneurship, and workplace productivity. They provide practical lessons for communication, personal development, and professional growth.

Seth Godin · Marketing and creativity

This talk explores how to make ideas stand out and reach the right audience, which is useful for marketing and entrepreneurship.

Why I recommend it: It helps explain how creative ideas can attract attention and connect with the people who are most likely to care about them.

Shawn Achor · Workplace and productivity

This talk discusses the connection between positive thinking, well-being, and how we approach our work.

Why I recommend it: It shows how a positive mindset can support productivity, improve our approach to challenges, and contribute to a healthier work environment.

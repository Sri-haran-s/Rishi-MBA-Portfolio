About Me

I completed my bachelor's degree in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at Sri Krishna College of Technology, Coimbatore. My undergraduate studies helped me build an interest in technology, data analysis and problem-solving.

I am currently pursuing an MBA at RV University. I want to understand how organisations make decisions and how technical knowledge can be applied to practical business problems. I am interested in developing my management, leadership and communication skills alongside my technical abilities.

My skills include data analysis, machine learning, Power BI and Python programming. I also value adaptability, decision-making, problem-solving, teamwork and collaboration. I want to keep improving these skills through coursework, projects and professional experience.

My career goal is to work in the IT industry as a Business Analyst or Data Analyst. I aim to apply Python, machine learning, data analysis and management knowledge to solve business problems, support data-driven decision-making and contribute to organisational growth. Over time, I want to take on greater responsibilities while continuing to improve my technical and communication skills.

This portfolio documents my assignments, learning and progress throughout my MBA.
